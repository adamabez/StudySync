Linked to Canvas

Link your Canvas account to see your homework here.

Where do I find it?
  1. Log in to Canvas and click Calendar in the left menu.
  2. At the bottom right, click Calendar Feed.
  3. Copy the link it shows and paste it here.

The feed has due dates but not what you've turned in, so tick off homework when you finish it. Keep the link private: anyone with it can see your calendar.

How do I get a token?
  1. Log in to Canvas and click Account, then Settings.
  2. Under Approved Integrations, click + New Access Token. (Some schools, like Sac State, turn this off. Use the Calendar Feed link instead.)
  3. Type "StudySync" as the purpose, then click Generate Token.
  4. Copy the token and paste it here. Canvas only shows it once.

With a token, StudySync can see what you turned in and your scores.

        Setting up StudySync?

        Create a file named .env in your StudySync folder containing AI_PROVIDER=gemini and GEMINI_API_KEY=your-key-here (free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey), then restart the server.

        Early access. Answers can take a little while, and the AI may be busy or error out when lots of people use it. If so, wait a minute and try again. It runs on Google Gemini's free tier, so don't use private or sensitive material: Google may use what you send to improve its products. The AI can make mistakes, so check important answers against your sources.
