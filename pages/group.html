      How do I get a webhook link?
      1. In Discord, hover over your group's channel and click the gear (Edit Channel).
      2. Open Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
      3. Click Copy Webhook URL and paste it here.

      You need permission to manage the channel. If you don't have it, ask whoever runs the class server.
