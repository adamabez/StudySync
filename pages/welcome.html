How it works

  1. 1

    Add your classes

    Pick them from the Sac State catalog and choose your section. Days and times fill in for you.

  2. 2

    Get a weekly study plan

    Set how many hours a week you want to study. StudySync puts sessions in the gaps around your classes.

  3. 3

    Meet your study group

    Want company? You're matched with up to 3 classmates in each class, at a weekly time you're all free.

Everything for the week in one place

Study groups that actually meet

Your group gets a weekly time when everyone is free. Each member taps Going, Running late or Can't make it, and if a week doesn't work, one tap moves it to a time that does.

A study plan that fits your week

Short, medium or long sessions, spread across your free time. Move or skip a session when plans change, and check it off when you're done.

Homework from Canvas

Link Canvas to see what's missing, what's due soon and what you've finished, right next to your schedule.

An AI study assistant

Add your notes or lecture PDFs and ask questions about them. Answers point to where in your materials they came from.
